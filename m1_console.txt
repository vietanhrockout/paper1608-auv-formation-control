============================================================
 STEP M.1 VERDICT
============================================================
1. Phi_i (Eq. 17 gradient basis) is provably independent of tau_i.
   Therefore swapping tau_cmd -> tau_act in critic_update/strategic_utility
   changes ONLY the scalar r_i (and thus c_e), not the direction of the
   critic update -- a minimal, surgical, well-justified candidate fix.
2. The reward/gradient inflation is concentrated at large initial |chi|
   (AUV0 t=0 ratio > 1e6) and collapses toward ~1x as |chi| shrinks,
   consistent with Issue K's observed onset at the very start of
   integration (Phase B.1 ode45 boundary crossing near t=0).
3. RECOMMENDATION: implement candidate fix under a new documented flag
   params.critic_reward_tau_mode in paper_params.m (values:
   'tau_cmd_raw' [current/literal] vs 'tau_act_saturated' [candidate]),
   wire rhs_3auv_rl.m to pass the selected quantity into critic_update,
   and re-run a Step K.2-style micro-horizon boundary-crossing check
   under both modes before altering the production default.
